The Gibbet of the Gem Warden

The heavy door slams shut behind you, and the echo takes far too long to die. Torchlight crawls across wet stone. In the center of the chamber stands a gibbet, and beside it, a figure in a cloak of crushed velvet the color of every gemstone at once.

"Three souls, one loop," the Warden says. "I hold a stone in my fist for each of you, drawn from the twelve months of the year. Name your stone from its riddle, letter by letter. Fail — any one of you — and the hour rewinds. You will stand at this door again, and again, until all three name their stones true."
